# SmartStudy Development Roadmap

## 🎯 Vision Statement

SmartStudy aims to become the leading AI-powered learning platform, transforming how students, educators, and professionals approach document-based learning. Our goal is to make learning more engaging, personalized, and effective through intelligent AI assistance.
